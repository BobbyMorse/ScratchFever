1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, including:

  • Email address and password when you create an account

We do not collect payment information. We do not sell lottery tickets.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and operate the ScratchFrenzy platform

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We may share data with service providers (e.g., hosting, analytics) who process data on our behalf under confidentiality agreements.

Inventory reports you submit are displayed publicly within the platform in aggregated form. Individual submissions are not attributed to your account in the public UI.

4. Cookies & Tracking

We use session cookies to keep you logged in. We may use analytics tools to understand platform usage. We do not use advertising trackers or sell behavioral data.

5. Data Retention

We retain account data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us at the address below.

6. Security

We use industry-standard security practices including password hashing and HTTPS. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

7. Children's Privacy

ScratchFrenzy is intended for users 18 years of age or older. We do not knowingly collect information from minors.

8. Changes to This Policy
