Delete Your Account

You can delete your ScratchFrenzy account and personal data at any time.

What gets deleted

  • Your email, username, and password hash
  • Your profile and preferences
  • Your saved ticket scan history
  • The link between your account and any inventory reports you submitted

Aggregated, anonymized data (e.g., "10 users reported Game X in stock") may remain in the platform but will no longer be tied to you. We retain a minimal log of the deletion request for audit purposes.

Option 1 — Delete from the Mobile App (fastest)

  1. Open the ScratchFrenzy app on iOS or Android.
  2. Tap the Profile tab.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm the deletion.

Your account will be deleted immediately and you will be signed out.

Option 2 — Delete from the Web

Sign in at scratchfrenzy.app, open your profile, and use the Delete Account control.

Option 3 — Email Request

Send a deletion request from the email address associated with your account:

Email a deletion request

We will confirm and complete the deletion within 30 days, typically within 1 business day.

About Active Subscriptions

Deleting your account does not automatically cancel an active Apple App Store or Google Play subscription. To stop future renewals:

  • iOS: App Store → your profile → Subscriptions → ScratchFrenzy → Cancel Subscription.
  • Android: Google Play → Account → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → ScratchFrenzy → Cancel.
